Our Story

Sundarya Studio was founded to give brides an unhurried, personal experience — every look is planned in advance and refined until it feels like you, only more radiant.

Our artists train in both classical Indian bridal technique and modern skincare-first makeup, so every result photographs beautifully and feels comfortable to wear all day.
